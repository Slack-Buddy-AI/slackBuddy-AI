# Employee Handbook (Sample - Fictional Company: Acme Tech)

## Working hours
Core working hours are 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM, Monday to Friday. Standard working time is 8 hours a day with a 1-hour lunch break.

## Dress code
Business casual on weekdays. Casual dress on Fridays.

## Holidays
The company observes 12 public holidays per year. The list is published each January on the HR portal.

## Who to contact
HR queries: hr@example.com. IT queries: #help-it on Slack.
