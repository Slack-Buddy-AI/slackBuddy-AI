# IT Setup Guide (Sample - Fictional Company: Acme Tech)

## Laptop
New joiners receive a laptop on day one from the IT desk. Sign the asset form when collecting it.

## Accounts
Your company email, Slack, and Git access are created before your joining date. Reset the temporary password on first login.

## VPN
Install the company VPN client from the IT portal. Use your company email and the one-time code sent to your phone.

## Getting help
Post in #help-it on Slack or email it-support@example.com. Response time is within 4 working hours.
