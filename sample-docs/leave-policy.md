# Leave Policy (Sample - Fictional Company: Acme Tech)

## Annual leave
Employees get 18 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, credited monthly.

## Sick leave
12 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required for absences of 3 or more consecutive days.

## Applying for leave
Apply through the HR portal at least 3 working days in advance, except for emergencies. Your manager approves requests within 2 working days.

## Probation
Employees on probation can take up to 1 day of leave per month.
